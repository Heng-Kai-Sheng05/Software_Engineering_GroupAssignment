— About The Hotel

A modern retreat in the
heart of Subang Jaya

Hotel Quest is a contemporary 4-star lifestyle hotel located in the vibrant township of Subang Jaya, Selangor. Recognised for its spacious accommodations, diverse dining options, and excellent facilities, we serve both leisure guests seeking relaxation and business travellers requiring professional services.

Strategically located near the SS15 LRT Station, the hotel provides easy access for domestic and international travellers. Discover the cultural treasures of Movie Animation Park Studios (MAPS) and the Sunway Pyramid, all within reach.
